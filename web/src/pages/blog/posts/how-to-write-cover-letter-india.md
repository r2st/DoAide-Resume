Do Indian companies even read cover letters? More often than candidates think. On Naukri, the "cover letter" box is optional but recruiters use it to break ties; on LinkedIn Easy Apply, your message to the recruiter plays the same role; for direct applications to startups and MNCs, a tailored email *is* your cover letter. This guide covers how to write one that works in the Indian market — fast.

## When you actually need a cover letter in India

- **Direct email applications** — always. Your email body is the cover letter.
- **Campus placements and off-campus drives** — usually not required, but a short one attached as page 2 of your PDF can differentiate you.
- **Naukri / Indeed applications** — fill the cover letter/summary box with a short version (120-150 words). Many recruiters filter by it.
- **LinkedIn** — use the "message to the hiring team" or a connection request note as a micro cover letter (60-80 words).
- **Referrals** — send the referrer a short paragraph they can forward. Make it effortless for them.

## The format that works (300 words, 4 paragraphs)

Indian recruiters spend under a minute on a cover letter. Use this structure:

**Paragraph 1 — Why this role (2-3 lines).** Name the exact role and company. One line on why this company specifically — a product you use, a recent launch, their market position. Generic praise ("your esteemed organisation") is skipped; specifics are read.

**Paragraph 2 — Your strongest proof (3-4 lines).** Your single most relevant achievement, with numbers. Match it to the top requirement in the job description. If the JD leads with "experience building REST APIs", your paragraph leads with the API you built and its scale.

**Paragraph 3 — Fit and context (2-3 lines).** Notice period, location/relocation willingness, and anything the recruiter would otherwise have to ask. In India, stating "serving 30-day notice, can join by [date]" answers the recruiter's first screening question before they ask it.

**Paragraph 4 — Close (1-2 lines).** Ask for the conversation. "I'd welcome a chance to discuss how I can contribute to [team/goal]. Available for a call any weekday." Then "Regards, [Name], [phone], [email]".

## What freshers should write instead of experience

Freshers can't lead with work experience — lead with evidence of ability:

- **Projects over coursework.** "Built a expense-tracker app with 500+ downloads" beats "studied Android development".
- **Internships, even short ones.** One concrete deliverable from a 2-month internship is a full paragraph.
- **Placements context.** If applying off-campus, say so plainly and state your availability — "2026 B.Tech graduate, available to join immediately" is information recruiters need.
- **Certifications that match the JD.** Name the specific certification, not "various online courses".

Avoid the fresher clichés: "hardworking and dedicated", "quick learner", "seeking a platform to prove myself". Every rejected cover letter in India contains these lines; they carry zero information. One specific project does more than all of them combined.

## Salary expectations: handle with care

If the application asks for current/expected CTC, answer factually — recruiters in India filter on it, and leaving it blank can drop you from the pipeline. In the cover letter itself, don't volunteer salary; "as per industry standards" in writing is fine if asked, and keep negotiation for the HR round.

## Five mistakes that get Indian cover letters rejected

1. **One letter for every company.** Recruiters recognise templates instantly. Changing only the company name — and missing one, so the letter says the wrong company — is the most common fatal error.
2. **Repeating your resume.** The letter should add context (why you, why them, logistics), not restate the same bullets.
3. **Overlong letters.** Anything past 350 words is skimmed or skipped.
4. **Flowery openings.** "Respected Sir/Madam, I humbly submit my candidature" reads as outdated. "Dear Hiring Manager" and plain language read as professional.
5. **No contact details or notice period.** Making a recruiter dig for your phone number or availability adds friction you can't afford.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a cover letter necessary for Naukri applications?

Not mandatory, but the cover-letter/summary field is read by recruiters shortlisting between similar profiles. A tight 120-150 word version tailored to the role measurably improves response rates compared to leaving it blank.

### How long should a cover letter be in India?

250-350 words for email and attached letters; 120-150 words for portal text boxes; 60-80 words for LinkedIn notes. Shorter and specific always beats longer and generic.

### Should freshers mention their percentage or CGPA?

Only if it's strong (above 7.5 CGPA or 70%) or the employer asks. Otherwise lead with projects, internships and certifications — they say more about job-readiness.

### Should I use AI to write my cover letter?

Use AI for the first draft and structure, then edit in the specifics: the company detail, your real numbers, your notice period. Sending an unedited AI draft is the new version of the copy-paste template — recruiters spot it.

### Do I address it to "Sir/Madam"?

"Dear Hiring Manager" is the safe modern default. If you know the recruiter's name (often visible on Naukri and LinkedIn postings), use it — "Dear Priya" or "Dear Ms. Sharma" both work.
