Every year, lakhs of engineering, BCA, MCA and B.Sc graduates apply to India's large IT services companies: TCS, Infosys, Wipro, HCLTech, Cognizant, Tech Mahindra, Accenture, Capgemini, LTIMindtree and others. Most freshers enter through campus placements or national hiring tests, and your **resume for TCS, Infosys or Wipro** comes into play at two points: shortlisting and the technical/HR interview.

This guide shows you how to write a fresher resume that works for IT services hiring in 2026. It covers format, what to include, sample bullets and the mistakes that commonly hurt freshers.

## How IT services hiring uses your resume

The exact process differs by company and year, but it usually looks like this:

1. **Registration and application** on the company's careers portal or hiring platform (for example, TCS candidates typically register via TCS NextStep for TCS NQT).
2. **Online assessment:** aptitude, logical reasoning, verbal ability and coding.
3. **Technical interview:** questions on your projects, programming basics, DBMS, OOP, and whatever you listed on your resume.
4. **HR / managerial interview:** communication, relocation, shift flexibility, bond or service agreement terms.

The key insight: **interviewers ask questions from your resume.** Every skill and project you list is a potential question. A short, honest resume beats a long, inflated one.

## The ideal fresher resume format for IT services

Keep it to **one page**, single column, in a clean and readable font.

| Order | Section | What to include |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Header | Name, phone, professional email, city, LinkedIn, GitHub |
| 2 | Summary | 2-3 lines: degree, core skills, what you want to do |
| 3 | Education | Degree, college, year, CGPA; Class 12 & 10 with % (often asked in campus hiring) |
| 4 | Technical skills | Languages, databases, tools, fundamentals |
| 5 | Projects | 2-3 projects with tech stack and your role |
| 6 | Internships / training | If any |
| 7 | Certifications | NPTEL, cloud fundamentals, HackerRank, etc. |
| 8 | Achievements & activities | Hackathons, coding contests, college clubs |

> **Education first.** For freshers, education goes above projects because recruiters check eligibility (degree, year, percentage criteria) first.

## What to put in the skills section

Group skills so the interviewer can scan them quickly:

- **Programming:** Java, Python, C
- **Web:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React (basic)
- **Databases:** MySQL, SQL queries, normalisation
- **Fundamentals:** OOP, data structures, DBMS, operating systems, computer networks
- **Tools:** Git, GitHub, VS Code, Eclipse
- **Cloud (if learned):** AWS Cloud Practitioner fundamentals

**Rule:** if you can't write a basic program or answer three interview questions on a skill, remove it.

## How to write project descriptions

Projects are where freshers stand out. Use this formula for each one: **what you built + tech used + your role + result or scale.**

**Weak:**
"Library Management System – made using Java."

**Strong:**
"Library Management System (Java, MySQL, JDBC): built a desktop app with issue/return tracking and fine calculation for 500+ book records; designed the normalised database schema and wrote all SQL queries. Team of 3."

More sample bullets:

- "Developed a student attendance web app (React, Node.js, MongoDB) used by 2 classes for one semester; implemented login and monthly reports."
- "Built a Python script to clean and analyse 10,000 rows of sales data with pandas; visualised trends with Matplotlib for a college data analytics course."
- "Trained a basic image classifier (Python, TensorFlow) on a public dataset as a final-year project; documented accuracy and limitations."

Be ready to explain every line, including what you would do differently.

## Summary examples for IT freshers

- "B.Tech (CSE) 2026 graduate with strong fundamentals in Java, OOP and SQL. Built 3 academic projects including a full-stack attendance app. Eager to start as a software engineer in application development."
- "BCA 2026 graduate skilled in Python, SQL and data visualisation, with an internship in data cleaning and reporting. Looking for a trainee role in data or testing."

Avoid "seeking a challenging position in a reputed organisation". It says nothing about you.

## Common mistakes that hurt IT freshers

1. **Copying a senior's or a friend's resume.** Interviewers see identical projects every day.
2. **Listing 20 technologies.** It invites tough questions and looks unfocused.
3. **Two-page resumes** with hobbies, a declaration and father's name.
4. **Unprofessional email IDs** like rockstar_rahul99@...
5. **Mismatched marks or dates** between your resume and application form, which can cause problems at verification.
6. **No GitHub or project links** when you claim coding projects.
7. **Typos in technical terms** ("Jave", "SQl", "Pyhton").

Your resume opens the door, but the interview decides the offer. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best resume format for a TCS or Infosys fresher?

A one-page, single-column resume with education first, followed by technical skills, two or three well-described projects, internships, certifications and achievements. Keep it simple and make sure you can explain everything on it.

### Should I include Class 10 and 12 percentages?

For IT services campus and off-campus hiring, yes, it's common to include them because many companies have eligibility criteria based on academic scores. Make sure they match the marks on your certificates.

### How many projects should a fresher resume have?

Two or three well-described projects are better than five one-liners. Choose ones you built yourself and can discuss in detail, ideally with a GitHub link.

### Do IT services companies use ATS?

Large employers typically use online application portals and hiring platforms that store and screen candidate data. A clean, text-based resume with standard section headings works best with these systems.

### Can I use the same resume for all IT companies?

You can keep one base resume, but tweak your summary and skills order to match each role, such as development, testing, support or data, and check it against the job description.
